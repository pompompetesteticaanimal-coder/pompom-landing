# Fotos embrulhadas no tamanho das fotos originais

Imagens em estilo fotográfico geradas com `image_gen` integrado; quatro arquivos opacos e retangulares que preenchem as molduras de ponta a ponta. São placeholders de fotos ainda não produzidas.

- `vermelho-dourado.png`: papel vermelho e fita dourada.
- `kraft-natural.png`: papel kraft e fita vermelha.
- `3d-divertido.png`: papel vermelho com patinhas, fotografado realisticamente (nome técnico preservado para compatibilidade).
- `branco-vermelho.png`: papel claro e laço vermelho.

O CSS reproduz as dimensões originais do carrossel Halloween: 4:3 nas fotos comuns e 16:9 na primeira foto do card de lembranças; mesmos espaçamentos, quantidade e molduras. A faixa “Em breve” continua em HTML nos três cards. As imagens anteriores de pequenas caixas isoladas ficam preservadas na pasta superior e não são mais usadas pelas prévias.

## Prompt-base

Make a realistic photographic image to REPLACE a rectangular photo on a Christmas pet-shop website. Landscape 4:3 composition. Subject: a FLAT RECTANGULAR PHOTO PRINT that has been completely gift-wrapped, filling the entire image edge to edge with [material below]. Full-bleed macro product photograph of the wrapping: the paper surface is the photo background and touches ALL FOUR EDGES. The concealed photo cannot be seen. This is NOT a small freestanding cubic gift box and NOT an isolated object. No empty background, no transparent background, no empty margins, no floor, no tabletop, no scene around the gift, no thick gold frame. Keep the photographic rectangular outline/frame implied by a tiny folded paper edge and shallow relief near bottom/right. Real paper grain, convincing satin fabric texture, folds, bow loops, natural soft shadows and highlights giving physical depth. Straight-on overhead camera, professional warm studio lighting, detailed natural materials. Leave the lower-middle paper surface quiet for an HTML 'Em breve' banner to be added later. Do not write any text, letters, logo or watermark. No dogs, cats, humans or visible photograph beneath paper. Whole image must look like an actual photographed wrapped rectangular photo panel, NOT an icon, vector or illustration.

## Materiais

1. Rich Christmas RED embossed wrapping paper, a broad woven GOLD satin ribbon crossing diagonally and a realistic tied gold bow near upper-left, subtle gold star print.
2. Natural textured KRAFT wrapping paper, a broad bright CHRISTMAS RED satin ribbon crossing the paper with an authentic dimensional bow at upper-left, small fresh evergreen sprig tucked into ribbon.
3. Christmas RED wrapping paper printed with small CREAM pet paw prints, a broad GOLD satin ribbon and real voluminous fabric bow toward upper-left, charming festive premium pet-shop gift wrap photographed realistically, NOT cartoon and NOT a 3D rendering.
4. Warm IVORY WHITE subtly embossed wrapping paper, a broad vibrant CHRISTMAS RED satin ribbon crossing diagonally and a real large satin bow near upper-left, one tiny gold bell with a small pine sprig.
