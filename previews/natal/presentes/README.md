# Caixas de presente — opções para os três cards de Natal

Quatro imagens originais geradas pela ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, com fundo transparente, mantidas localmente nesta pasta:

1. `vermelho-dourado.png`: veludo vermelho e laço dourado.
2. `kraft-natural.png`: papel kraft, fita vermelha, pinho e pinha.
3. `3d-divertido.png`: 3D lúdico vermelho e dourado com patinhas.
4. `branco-vermelho.png`: caixa clara, laço vermelho, sino e pinho.

Não são fotos dos futuros produtos ou brindes da PomPom. São ilustrações de presentes que ocultam o conteúdo ainda não produzido. Todas recebem faixa “Em breve” em HTML. O laboratório permite comparar quatro telas lado a lado, escolher o estilo e testar os três cards no celular virtual.

## Prompt-base

Create one high quality standalone Christmas GIFT BOX asset for a Brazilian pet shop website's coming-soon campaign. [Style modifier below.] Composition: one single complete gift box centered, full box and ribbon inside image with at least 10 percent clear breathing room on all sides; three-quarter product view, no cropped corners, square image. Isolated on a genuinely transparent alpha background, with only a soft semitransparent contact shadow. No room, floor plane, backdrop, human, animal or extra packages. Distinct material and box design as described, red must remain prominent even in kraft/ivory styles. No typography, no logo, NO text, no watermark, no label: we will add the Portuguese 'Em breve' ribbon as accessible HTML in the website. Real photographic object for photo styles; charming clay render only for the clay style. Not a flat wrapping texture, not a golden picture frame, not a collage.

## Modificadores usados

- **Vermelho e dourado:** Luxury Christmas product photography of one closed rigid RED velvet square gift box, substantial deep red lid, large sculptural GOLD satin ribbon bow, realistic fabric weave, elegant three-quarter camera showing lid and two sides, warm highlights and grounded soft shadow, small restrained evergreen sprig with two red berries tucked into the bow.
- **Kraft:** Warm editorial Christmas product photography of one closed KRAFT PAPER square gift box, natural matte brown textured paper with folded corners, a rich RED satin ribbon tied in a lovely loose bow, small fresh pine sprig, two red berries and a tiny pinecone under the bow, three-quarter camera showing top and two sides, cozy artisanal aesthetic, realistic paper and fabric.
- **3D lúdico:** Charming high quality stylized 3D clay render of one chunky rounded RED Christmas gift box, creamy ivory paw-print pattern (simple shapes, not letters), oversized thick soft GOLD bow on lid, charming toy-like proportions, matte soft-touch surface, polished ambient occlusion, three-quarter perspective showing box depth. Tiny evergreen accent. Clearly a freestanding cubic gift box, not a photo frame.
- **Claro e vermelho:** Minimal premium Christmas studio photography of one closed IVORY WHITE rectangular rigid gift box, beautifully folded matte ivory paper, very wide bright CHRISTMAS RED satin ribbon forming a lush dimensional bow on top, delicate small gold bell and tiny evergreen sprig tied under bow, three-quarter camera with generous lid and visible sides, elegant airy gift boutique aesthetic, tangible depth and soft warm light.
